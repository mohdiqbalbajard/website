Our story

Iqbal Global Group was founded in 2026 at Kupwara, Jammu & Kashmir, on a plain conviction: that the young talent of the valley, properly trained and honestly led, can run world-class operations for the world's largest freight market.

The Group works through two companies in concert: Iqbal Outsourcing India Private Limited, the operating company at Kupwara where the work is done, and Iqbal Outsourcing LLC of Wyoming, United States, which faces the American market we serve.

The Group is led by its founding Managing Director, Mohd Iqbal Bajard.
